# Consumer-credit publication rules

- Treat every result as retrospective research evidence, never a credit decision, eligibility test, price, or adverse-action reason.
- Exclude demographic fields from model inputs and public individual records. Keep the approved fairness review local and aggregate-only.
- Separate ranking, calibration, and review-capacity evidence; none establishes operational lending readiness.
- State the source vintage and validation limits beside performance claims. Do not imply current-market or out-of-time performance.
- Describe feature-group ablations as model-reliance checks, never causes or explanations about a person.
