# 0016 Director risk control surface

## Decision

Make the validation view lead with an explicit research-only disposition, open evidence gates, missing accountability fields, and service-verification state. Prevent invalid numeric ranges from changing the cohort, clear stale record context after filter changes, and express record placement as minimum simulated capacity rather than a binary decision-like state.

## Why

The workstation already made model evidence challengeable, but a risk executive still had to synthesize the decision across dense tables. Numeric ranges could also create contradictory states, and record-level inside or outside language resembled operational decisioning despite the research boundary.

## Alternatives rejected

- A new governance backend was rejected because the public artifact contains no approved owner, reviewer, due date, or institutional disposition to serve.
- A generated committee PDF was rejected because the browser print path provides a reviewable brief without another dependency or document pipeline.
- Hiding missing evidence was rejected because absent temporal validation, accountability, and service verification must remain visible.

## Not done

No owner, reviewer, approval date, review date, or production claim was invented. No public deployment, provider teardown, redirect, push, release, or publication was performed. Retirement of the superseded Cloudflare surface still requires the owning account and explicit approval.

## Changed

Validation now opens with a disposition register and evidence gates before supporting metrics. Detailed development tables use progressive disclosure. Numeric range edits validate and apply together. Record inspectors clear when filters exclude the selected record, record search and empty states are distinct, page navigation accepts a direct page number, count intervals use whole records, and capacity sensitivity replaces binary placement emphasis.
