# Case-study claim standard

The case study may describe the stakeholder problem, licensed-data provenance, privacy policy, leakage controls, reproducible evaluation, analyst workflow, local M10 verification, and exact deployed revision only when each fact links to `.project/evidence.yml` or an immutable artifact.

Required language:

- “Retrospective academic benchmark” and “research only.”
- Fixed stratified holdout, not out-of-time validation.
- No automated approval, denial, pricing, adverse-action, or lending recommendation. Record-level language may describe only inclusion or exclusion from a score-ranked simulated research review set.
- A model or deployment date is not a data-vintage claim; distinguish source vintage, evaluation generation time, evaluated revision, and live verification time.
- Protected attributes excluded from model inputs and public individual analytics.
- Reported credit limit is not exposure, loss, or balance.

Prohibited claims include production readiness, live consumer use, causal impact, loss prevention, revenue, approval quality, operational staffing savings, forecast accuracy, or portfolio-ready visual acceptance before owner approval. Never call simulated review placement `the model's decision`, approval, denial, acceptance, rejection, eligibility, or adverse-action evidence. A disclaimer does not make those labels permissible. Local screenshots and candidate bundle metrics must be labeled local until deployed and verified.
